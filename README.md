# Hensal house explorer

**R5:** the whole space under the upper flight is one built-in unit. Measured from the stair-side face of the bedroom wall: a store fills the 1.47 m-high zone under the landing (0–1.40 m, about 2.6 m² and 3.7 m³, bifold doors on the passage face beside the bedroom door), a mirror partition stands at 1.40 m, a semi-recessed basin sits at 1.40–1.75 m and the user stands at 1.75–2.35 m facing west with 2.17–2.53 m overhead. The TV panel, the rectangular cabinet and the staircase are unchanged; the former 600 mm cabinet opening is closed by a fixed oak panel on the TV line. The only architectural change is the 900 × 1400 mm store-door opening in the stair-side partition, which the structural engineer must confirm (fallback: mirror-fronted door, no wall change). PDF sheets 03 and 07 and the model are updated.

Open [the interactive 3D model](drawings/compact-v4/interactive-3d.html) in a modern browser, or in the desktop Preview tab. It is a self-contained offline HTML file; no install or server is required.

Orbit, zoom and pan around the house; isolate the ground floor, first floor or roof; lower walls; inspect a cutaway or separated floors; select rooms; toggle furniture and dimensions; save a PNG. The current 11 plan sheets and complete downloadable PDF are embedded.

The dimensioned review set governs the model. Unspecified heights, illustrative finishes and source discrepancies are listed in **Model assumptions & source notes** (the ⓘ button on narrow screens). This visualization does not resolve construction detailing that remains unspecified in the source.
