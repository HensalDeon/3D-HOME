ACCURACY & TRACEABILITY

What the model is based on

The dimensioned vectors govern. The unchanged parts follow the coordinated PDF. R1 applies only the requested living/storage, basin, Bedroom 3 wardrobe and front-band revisions. The original front photograph now governs the two charcoal bands. R2 established the stair geometry. R4 integrated the TV at the stair boundary. R5 turns the under-stair space into one built-in unit: a store under the intermediate landing, a mirror partition and a west-facing basin. The stair and all approved architecture remain fixed, apart from the store-door opening in the stair-side partition, which needs structural confirmation.

Carried directly from the drawings

6.00 × 9.70 m envelope; unchanged room walls and all door/window positions; 150 mm external and 100 mm internal walls; three private ensuites; room and furniture footprints; side-access rear balcony; revised ground starter, retained upper flight and existing roof stair; roof enclosure, setbacks, finished levels, front and rear scheduled opening heights; roof guarding and screen levels.

Visual assumptions

Main slabs are shown at an assumed 150 mm and main-storey clear walls at 2.85 m. Interior doors use an assumed 2.10 m height unless scheduled. Side-window sills/heights are inferred: generally 1.00 / 1.20 m, with a high-level bathroom window. Furniture heights, joinery, material textures, frame depths, rail sections, planting, boundary heights and car styling are illustrative. Thin surface skins use a 3 mm rendering offset to avoid flicker; they do not revise the dimensioned envelope. Structural beams and columns have not been invented.

Details awaiting coordination in the source

The plot is a nominal 9.70 × 16.80 m rectangle; the surveyed plot is slightly skewed. Parking turning clearance, exact bearing, well and septic siting, stair thickness and walking-line headroom, guard anchors, cladding build-up, drainage falls, waterproofing and services remain unresolved. The services box is a reserve, not an installed tank.

Drawing conflicts made explicit

The rear ground service door is positioned at y = 8.20 m by the floor plan, although the elevation projects it to the rear façade. It is modeled in the recessed wall. The first-floor north bedroom has no opening in its long passage wall in the source, so its access is retained through the front gallery. The roof plan places both enclosure vents at x = 0.55 m; the rear elevation implies a 0.20 m lateral shift. The model follows the roof plan. The 900 mm roof-exit leaf swings into the indicated 1.10 m landing reserve; that reserve is not an additional raised platform.

This is a design-review visualization. The source is not a signed construction set, so an error-free or construction-certified model cannot be claimed.
R5 · UNDER-STAIR BUILT-IN

One built-in unit under the stair

R5 uses the whole space beneath the upper flight as one built-in unit. Measured from the stair-side face of the bedroom wall: store 0–1.40 m, mirror partition at 1.40 m, semi-recessed basin 1.40–1.75 m, standing zone 1.75–2.35 m. The 1.47 m-high zone under the landing is storage; the person stands only where the soffit is 2.17 m or higher. The complete R2 staircase, upper connection, kitchen, bedrooms and every other wall, door and window remain fixed.

TV centered on the OLD cabinet span

The removed sloping cabinets extended from 3.60 to 4.65 m along the stair. Their midpoint, 4.125 m, sets the TV center exactly. The screen center is now 1.10 m above the living floor. The TV is mounted on a 50 mm slim backing panel at the stair/living boundary. Both backing and screen stay within that boundary, leaving the living/dining floor open. The pole and freestanding stand have been removed. The TV does not follow the new storage toward the high end.

Under-landing store · 0 to 1.40 m from the bedroom wall

The 1.90 × 1.35 m floor beneath the intermediate landing, its 500 mm extension and risers 10–11 becomes a built-in store of about 2.6 m² and 3.7 m³, against 0.3 m³ in the cabinet. The carcass top is 1.42 m under the landing and steps up to 1.59 and 1.77 m under the flight, 45–55 mm below each soffit. Two 450 mm bifold leaves, 1.40 m high, open from the passage face beside the bedroom door, where the person stands under the full 2.85 m slab. The pocket under the lower landing is crouch-in bulk storage. The 900 × 1400 mm opening is cut in the retained 100 mm stair-side partition, leaving the wall above; the structural engineer must confirm the landing does not bear on it. If the wall cannot be opened, the partition becomes a mirror-fronted door and the store is a crouch-in cupboard from the wash side, with no wall change.

Stepped vanity · user faces WEST

A mirror partition stands 1.40 m from the bedroom wall, top +1.75 m under the 1.82 m soffit, aligned with the cabinet's west face and the landing-extension edge. The 500 × 350 mm semi-recessed basin sits on a 350 mm counter with a drawer, rim +0.86 m, against the back of the TV panel. Its front edge is 1.75 m from the wall, 0.53 m closer than R4 and the westernmost usable line. The 750 × 600 mm standing zone runs 1.75–2.35 m from the wall: 2.17 m clear over the 150 mm leaning strip (30 mm under the 2.20 m benchmark, disclosed), 2.35 m over the body line and 2.53 m at the rear, with 2.00 m over the bowl. Figures use the modeled 120 mm stair zone; a thicker waist slab reduces each by the difference, and a front edge at 2.00 m keeps 2.35 m throughout. Access uses the existing 900 mm stair-entry opening behind the TV panel. Hand washing only.

Cabinet and closing panel

The 500 × 550 × 1080 mm cabinet stays beneath the last two lower-flight treads, flat-topped with two 250 mm doors. Its doors are now reached from the wash side through a 400 mm strip beside the basin under a 2.00–2.17 m soffit: stoop access to a low cabinet, not a passage. The former 600 mm passage opening is closed by a fixed 50 mm oak panel, 1.70 m high, continuing the TV backing so the passage reads as one joinery wall: TV, panel, store doors.

Bedroom 3 wardrobe

The original cabinet stays at its existing position. A 550 mm deep, 2.10 m high oak return runs along the adjoining entrance-side wall. Paired corner doors open without a fixed corner post. The entrance door, north window, bed and bedroom dimensions remain unchanged.

Front charcoal bands

The first-floor ribbon now has the reference's open upper-left termination and a separate lower return at the balcony. Both bands use custom cubic curves with broad, asymmetric sweeps and a slimmer 145 mm visible face. Their 80 mm visual projection is retained. Neither band relocates an opening, railing or slab.

Lower turn and retained upper connection

The practical route is SOUTH → WEST → EAST: 2 + 7 + 8 risers, with 900 mm flights and 250 mm treads. Only the lower side of the intermediate landing extends by 500 mm. The upper eight risers, first-floor arrival, floor opening and roof stair remain fixed. The exact NORTH → SOUTH → NORTH → EAST sequence is not used.

The kitchen stays 2.90 × 2.05 m. The previously studied 500 mm gain would overlap the stair-entry landing with the wash zone. Keeping both facilities beneath the stairs and preserving proper access takes priority.

The complete PDF and embedded plan library include R5. Other rooms and architectural elements retain their previous designs. Stair construction, the store-door opening, fixture mounting, finishes and plumbing remain for project detailing.
