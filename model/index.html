ACCURACY & TRACEABILITY

What the model is based on

The dimensioned vectors govern. The unchanged parts follow the coordinated PDF. R1 applies only the requested living/storage, basin, Bedroom 3 wardrobe and front-band revisions. The original front photograph now governs the two charcoal bands. R2 established the stair geometry. R4 integrated the TV at the stair boundary. R5 turns the under-stair space into one built-in unit: a store under the intermediate landing, a mirror partition and a west-facing basin. R6 represents the stair as a conceptual cast-in-situ RCC waist-slab system and re-checks every joinery top against the new soffit. The stair geometry and all approved architecture remain fixed, apart from the store-door opening in the stair-side partition.

Carried directly from the drawings

6.00 × 9.70 m envelope; unchanged room walls and all door/window positions; 150 mm external and 100 mm internal walls; three private ensuites; room and furniture footprints; side-access rear balcony; revised ground starter, retained upper flight and existing roof stair; roof enclosure, setbacks, finished levels, front and rear scheduled opening heights; roof guarding and screen levels.

Visual assumptions

Main slabs are shown at an assumed 150 mm and main-storey clear walls at 2.85 m. Interior doors use an assumed 2.10 m height unless scheduled. Side-window sills/heights are inferred: generally 1.00 / 1.20 m, with a high-level bathroom window. Furniture heights, joinery, material textures, frame depths, rail sections, planting, boundary heights and car styling are illustrative. Thin surface skins use a 3 mm rendering offset to avoid flicker; they do not revise the dimensioned envelope. The only structure shown is the conceptual RCC stair: an indicative 150 mm waist, 150 mm landing slabs and two support zones drawn inside walls and slab edges that already exist. No reinforcement, bar size, spacing, beam size, column size or footing is implied, and no other beams or columns have been invented.

Details awaiting coordination in the source

The plot is a nominal 9.70 × 16.80 m rectangle; the surveyed plot is slightly skewed. Parking turning clearance, exact bearing, well and septic siting, the final stair waist thickness, landing beams, supports and connections, guard anchors, cladding build-up, drainage falls, waterproofing and services remain unresolved. The services box is a reserve, not an installed tank.

Drawing conflicts made explicit

The rear ground service door is positioned at y = 8.20 m by the floor plan, although the elevation projects it to the rear façade. It is modeled in the recessed wall. The first-floor north bedroom has no opening in its long passage wall in the source, so its access is retained through the front gallery. The roof plan places both enclosure vents at x = 0.55 m; the rear elevation implies a 0.20 m lateral shift. The model follows the roof plan. The 900 mm roof-exit leaf swings into the indicated 1.10 m landing reserve; that reserve is not an additional raised platform.

This is a design-review visualization. The source is not a signed construction set, so an error-free or construction-certified model cannot be claimed.
R6 · CONCEPTUAL RCC STAIR

One built-in unit under a conceptual RCC stair

R6 keeps the R5 built-in unit exactly where it is and replaces the modelled separate treads with a conventional cast-in-situ RCC staircase: a continuous waist slab under each flight, monolithic with the landing slabs. Measured from the stair-side face of the bedroom wall, nothing moves in plan: store 0–1.40 m, mirror partition at 1.40 m, semi-recessed basin 1.40–1.75 m, standing zone 1.75–2.35 m. A waist soffit is an inclined plane rather than a flat lid under each tread, so it sits lower and the joinery tops come down with it: the zone under the landing is 1.42 m and the person stands under 1.98–2.40 m. The complete R2 staircase, riser and tread geometry, upper connection, kitchen, bedrooms and every other wall, door and window remain fixed.

Conceptual RCC stair structure

STAIR SHOWN AS CONCEPTUAL RCC WAIST-SLAB SYSTEM. FINAL WAIST-SLAB THICKNESS, LANDING BEAMS, SUPPORT CONDITIONS, REINFORCEMENT AND CONNECTIONS TO BE DESIGNED BY STRUCTURAL ENGINEER. 100 MM PARTITION WALLS ARE NOT TO BE ASSUMED LOAD-BEARING.

The massing shown is a folded plate: an indicative 150 mm waist under each 900 mm flight, its top plane on the steps' internal corners, monolithic with 150 mm landing slabs at the turns, with the 20 mm stone finish as a separate layer over the concrete so the walking levels never move. Support is taken only from elements that already exist — the plinth under the starter landing, the 150 mm west external wall, a landing beam zone inside the existing 100 mm bedroom cross-wall line, and the floor-slab trimmer at the stairwell edge. There are no cantilevered treads, no steel stringers, no wall below the upper flight and no new column in the living area. Thicknesses are conceptual massing only; no reinforcement, bar size, spacing, beam size, column size or footing is implied. The FF-to-roof stair repeats the same system.

TV centered on the OLD cabinet span

The removed sloping cabinets extended from 3.60 to 4.65 m along the stair. Their midpoint, 4.125 m, sets the TV center exactly. The screen center is now 1.10 m above the living floor. The TV is mounted on a 50 mm slim backing panel at the stair/living boundary. Both backing and screen stay within that boundary, leaving the living/dining floor open. The pole and freestanding stand have been removed. The TV does not follow the new storage toward the high end.

Under-landing store · 0 to 1.40 m from the bedroom wall

Against the RCC soffit the carcass top is 1.37 m under the landing slab, 1.33 m where the waist springs off it and 1.51 m under riser 11, still 45–55 mm below each soffit. Two 450 mm bifold leaves, 1.40 m high, still clear the 1.42 m landing soffit and open from the passage face beside the bedroom door, where the person stands under the full 2.85 m slab. The pocket under the lower landing is crouch-in bulk storage. The 900 × 1400 mm opening in the retained 100 mm stair-side partition is unchanged; because R6 carries the intermediate landing on a beam zone inside the bedroom cross-wall line instead, the cut no longer depends on that partition, but it and the whole support system still need the structural engineer. The fallback remains a mirror-fronted door and a crouch-in cupboard from the wash side, with no wall change.

Lower turn and retained upper connection

Only the lower side of the intermediate landing extends by 500 mm.
