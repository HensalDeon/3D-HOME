ACCURACY & TRACEABILITY

What the model is based on

The dimensioned vectors govern. This model follows the current coordinated PDF and its retained drawing coordinates. The photographs on sheet 10 are historical style references.

Carried directly from the drawings

6.00 × 9.70 m envelope; wall and aperture positions; 150 mm external and 100 mm internal walls; three private ensuites; room and furniture footprints; side-access rear balcony; full dog-leg stair on both storeys; roof enclosure, setbacks, finished levels, front and rear scheduled opening heights; roof guarding and screen levels.

Visual assumptions

Main slabs are shown at an assumed 150 mm and main-storey clear walls at 2.85 m. Interior doors use an assumed 2.10 m height unless scheduled. Side-window sills/heights are inferred: generally 1.00 / 1.20 m, with a high-level bathroom window. Furniture heights, joinery, material textures, frame depths, rail sections, planting, boundary heights and car styling are illustrative. Thin surface skins use a 3 mm rendering offset to avoid flicker; they do not revise the dimensioned envelope. Structural beams and columns have not been invented.

Details awaiting coordination in the source

The plot is a nominal 9.70 × 16.80 m rectangle; the surveyed plot is slightly skewed. Parking turning clearance, exact bearing, well and septic siting, stair thickness and walking-line headroom, guard anchors, cladding build-up, drainage falls, waterproofing and services remain unresolved. The services box is a reserve, not an installed tank.

Drawing conflicts made explicit

The rear ground service door is positioned at y = 8.20 m by the floor plan, although the elevation projects it to the rear façade. It is modeled in the recessed wall. The first-floor north bedroom has no opening in its long passage wall in the source, so its access is retained through the front gallery. The roof plan places both enclosure vents at x = 0.55 m; the rear elevation implies a 0.20 m lateral shift. The model follows the roof plan. The 900 mm roof-exit leaf swings into the indicated 1.10 m landing reserve; that reserve is not an additional raised platform.

This is a design-review visualization. The source is not a signed construction set, so an error-free or construction-certified model cannot be claimed.
